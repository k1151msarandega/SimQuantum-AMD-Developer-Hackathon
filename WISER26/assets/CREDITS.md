# Asset credits

WISER judges on documentation/reproducibility and expects proper licensing
for any reused material -- every non-original image in this repo must have
a line here before submission. Do not merge an asset without one.

## Folder convention
- `assets/hardware_refs/` -- real photos (cryostats, dilution fridges, actual chip/device photos)
- `assets/renders/` -- 3D/illustrative renders (Blender scenes, schematic device art)
